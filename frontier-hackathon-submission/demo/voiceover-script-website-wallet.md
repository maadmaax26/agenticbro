## Recording Notes

**Pacing:**
- Problem section: Slightly slower, serious tone
- Feature walkthrough: Steady, confident
- Demo section: Energetic, show excitement about the tech
- Closing: Clear, memorable

**Emphasis words:**
- BEFORE (in "before you sign")
- EVERY (in "every instruction")
- DON'T SIGN (urgent)
- FREE (in "free scans")

**Background:**
- Optional: Subtle tech/electronic music bed
- No music during demo narration
- Music swells at "Scan first. Trust later."
