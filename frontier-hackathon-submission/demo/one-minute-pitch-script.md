## Recording Tips

**Voiceover:**
- Fast but clear — practice pacing
- Emphasize numbers: "SEVENTEEN BILLION", "FIVE free scans"
- Pause slightly after "Agentic Bro fixes that" — let it land
- End with confidence, not desperation

**Text Overlays:**
- Bold, high-contrast colors
- Keep text under 6 words per overlay
- Sync with voiceover — text appears slightly before voice

**Pacing:**
- No shot longer than 5 seconds
- Quick cuts create urgency
- Slow down only for tagline
