## Phase 3: Token Mechanics Defense

### The "$100 Hold" Stabilizer Argument

**Problem they'll probe:** "What prevents users from buying $AGNTCBRO, getting scans, then immediately selling?"

**Answer:**
- Users must **maintain** the $100 hold to keep access. If they sell below threshold, scans stop next billing cycle.
- This is fundamentally different from pay-per-scan (high velocity, downward pressure).
- It creates a **structural demand floor** — every new user who wants scans must buy and hold.
- As scan volume grows, more supply is pulled out of open circulation.
- The hold requirement is the token's core value proposition, not speculation.

**Math:** 1,000 users holding $100 each = $100K in organic demand sitting in wallets, not on exchanges. Each new user adds net buying pressure without corresponding sell pressure.
