## Scam Tips (rotate daily)

### Tip 1: Fake Giveaways
🚨 **Scam Alert:** Fake giveaways are the #1 way scammers steal crypto.

**Red flags:**
- "Send 1 SOL, get 10 back"
- Celebrity accounts promising to double your crypto
- Urgent time limits ("Act now!")

**Rule:** If it sounds too good to be true, it's a scam. Real projects NEVER ask you to send crypto first.

🔐 Stay safe — scan before you trust.

---

### Tip 2: Fake Support Accounts
⚠️ **Warning:** Scammers impersonate support teams on Telegram and Discord.

**They'll:**
- DM you claiming there's a "problem with your wallet"
- Ask for your seed phrase or private key
- Create fake urgency

**Remember:** Real support will NEVER ask for your seed phrase. EVER.

🔐 If someone DMs you first, it's 99% a scam.

---

### Tip 3: Token Impersonation
🔍 **Spot check:** Scammers create fake tokens with similar names to real projects.

**Example:**
- Real: $AGNTCBRO (contract: 52bJEa5NDpJyDbzKFaRDLgRCxALGb15W86x4Hbzopump)
- Fake: $AGNTCBR0 (with zero), $AGNTBRO, $AGNTCBROv2

**Always verify the contract address.** Don't trust the ticker alone.

🔐 Check contracts at agenticbro.app

---

### Tip 4: Romance Scams / Pig Butchering
💔 **Heartless:** Romance scams (pig butchering) are on the rise.

**The playbook:**
1. Build relationship over weeks/months
2. Mention their "crypto success"
3. Offer to "help you invest"
4. Vanish after you send funds

**They're not your girlfriend/boyfriend. They're a scammer with a script.**

🔐 Never send crypto to someone you met online.

---

### Tip 5: Phishing Links
🔗 **Click carefully:** Phishing links steal your wallet with one click.

**Danger zones:**
- "Check your airdrop" links
- Fake wallet sites (Metamask, Phantom lookalikes)
- Shortened URLs hiding malicious domains

**Pro tip:** Hover before you click. Check the domain carefully.

🔐 When in doubt, don't click. Go directly to official sites.
